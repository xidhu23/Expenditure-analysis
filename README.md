# Expenditure-analysis
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes IT expenditure by comparing Actual, Forecast, and Planned costs across business areas, IT functions, and regions to identify budget variances, monitor spending performance, and support strategic financial decision-making.
This project focuses on analyzing and monitoring IT expenditure across multiple business areas, IT functions, and geographic regions using Power BI. The primary objective of the dashboard is to provide a clear and comprehensive view of organizational IT spending by comparing Actual, Forecast, and Planned costs, enabling stakeholders to assess financial performance and make informed budgeting decisions.

The dashboard consolidates IT cost data from various sources and presents it through interactive visualizations, KPI indicators, and detailed variance analyses. Users can evaluate how actual spending aligns with planned budgets, identify deviations from forecasts, and understand the financial impact of those variances across different dimensions of the business. Key metrics such as Actual vs Plan, Actual vs Forecast, and Forecast vs Plan help measure budget adherence and forecasting effectiveness.

The report enables in-depth analysis across major IT areas, including Infrastructure, Functional Services, Governance, Enablement, and Business Unit Support, helping management identify which functions contribute most to overall expenditure variances. Waterfall charts highlight the drivers behind budget deviations, making it easier to pinpoint areas of overspending or underspending and investigate underlying causes such as project delays, resource constraints, cost-saving initiatives, or unexpected operational expenses.

In addition to functional analysis, the dashboard provides regional insights by comparing expenditure performance across geographic locations. This allows stakeholders to identify regions with significant budget gaps, understand spending patterns, and evaluate whether investments are aligned with business priorities. Interactive filters for month, IT area, and business area enable users to drill down into specific segments and perform targeted analyses.

The dashboard also supports financial planning and performance management by highlighting trends in expenditure, budget utilization, and forecast accuracy. By bringing together actual, forecasted, and planned cost data in a single analytical view, the solution improves transparency, enhances budget control, and supports strategic decision-making. Ultimately, this analysis helps organizations optimize IT spending, improve forecasting reliability, allocate resources more effectively, and ensure that technology investments are aligned with overall business objectives.
